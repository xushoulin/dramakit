AI 短剧制作全流程：八步走通

更新于 2026-11 · 阅读约 6 分钟

2026 年第二季度，全国新上线的微短剧里 AI 生成作品占比已超过六成。一部过去需要编剧组两周的案头工作，现在一个工作周就能交付。这篇指南把整条链路拆成八步，每步给出"做什么 + 用什么"。

第 1 步：选题与钩子设计

短剧生死在前 3 秒。先定题材（战神归来 / 复仇打脸 / 先婚后爱），再写"每集一个钩子、三集一个大反转"的爆点分布表。让大模型做竞品分析和受众画像，人只负责立意。

第 2 步：剧本创作（注意：不是传统剧本）

传统剧本是给演员和导演看的；AI 短剧剧本必须面向机器生产。每一场戏都要有：场景 ID、出场角色、对白、镜头类型、预估时长。可用的 LLM：DeepSeek（逻辑严谨）、豆包（中文语境）、Kimi（长上下文，约 12.8 万 token 可撑 20-30 集）。

第 3 步：剧本工程化

把剧本转成结构化格式：项目总纲 + 人物圣经（可视化标签）+ 场景圣经 + 分镜表。这是决定成片一致性的关键一步——可以直接用我们的 剧本结构化转换器，粘贴文本即可生成含 videoPrompt 字段的分镜 JSON。原理见剧本工程化详解。

第 4 步：角色与场景资产设定

用图像模型（即梦、Midjourney、SD+ControlNet）生成角色定妆照和场景概念图。这些参考图之后要喂给视频模型锁定一致性——脸一旦变了，观众三秒弃剧。

第 5 步：分镜与视频生成

主流引擎：Seedance 2.5（字节系，30 秒连续生成 + 多模态参考）、可灵 3.0（动作与物理真实感）、海螺 H3（性价比量产）、Sora / Veo 3.1（海外项目）。写提示词建议用 Prompt 构建器按平台分别生成。

关键省钱技巧：分镜分级。远景过场用低分辨率抽卡，特写情绪镜头才上高配，渲染成本能降约 40%。

第 6 步：自动审片与重抽

抽卡可用率通常只有 30%，量产必须上自动审片（画面可用性、角色一致性、逻辑纠错），像巨日禄这类 Agent 能把可用率拉到 90%。个人创作者也可以人工只审关键镜头。

第 7 步：配音与音效

中文首选讯飞智作（拟真度最高、支持方言），英文用 ElevenLabs。Veo 3.1 和部分新模型支持原生音频输出，可以连台词一起生成。

第 8 步：剪辑与出海分发

剪映完成精剪、字幕、多平台导出。出海是增长最猛的口子：海外 AI 剧/漫剧市场规模同比暴涨 550%，多语种译制（趣丸、腾讯云一站式方案）已能批量完成，人只打磨文化禁忌与本土化细节。

工具选型一句话总结

  • 个人试水：小云雀（39 元/月）或免费 LLM + 结构化工作流
  • 连载团队：火山剧创 / LibTV / 万镜一刻 等全流程平台
  • 技术型团队：ComfyUI + FLUX + Wan2.2 本地部署，无水印

整个流程里，真正拉开差距的不是单镜画质，而是第 3 步的工程化程度。从 结构化你的剧本 开始。

The AI Short Drama Production Workflow in 8 Steps

Updated Nov 2026 · ~6 min read

By Q2 2026, AI-generated titles made up over 60% of newly released microdramas in China. Case work that once took a two-week writers' room now ships in under a week. Here is the full pipeline, broken into eight steps with tool picks for each.

Step 1: Concept & hook design

Short drama lives or dies in the first 3 seconds. Pick a genre (billionaire revenge / sweet-marriage / underdog rise), then plan a "hook per episode, big reversal every three episodes" beat sheet. Use LLMs for competitor analysis and audience profiling; keep the theme human.

Step 2: Scriptwriting (not a traditional script)

Traditional scripts are written for actors and directors. AI-drama scripts must be written for machines: every scene needs a scene ID, characters, dialogue, shot type and estimated duration. Good LLMs: Claude or GPT for creative range, Kimi or Gemini for long context (a 128k-token window covers roughly 20–30 episodes).

Step 3: Script engineering

Convert the script into a structured format: project bible + character bible (visual tags) + scene bible + shot list. This step decides consistency more than anything else — run your text through our Script Formatter to get storyboard JSON with ready-to-paste videoPrompt fields. See Script Engineering explained.

Step 4: Character & scene assets

Generate character reference sheets and concept art (Midjourney, Jimeng, SD + ControlNet). These references get fed into the video model to lock consistency — once faces drift, viewers bail in three seconds.

Step 5: Storyboarding & video generation

Main engines: Seedance 2.5 (30s continuous clips + multimodal reference), Kling 3.0 (physics-aware motion), Hailuo H3 (cost-effective volume), Sora / Veo 3.1 for western-facing projects. Generate per-platform prompts with our Prompt Builder.

Cost trick: shot tiering. Render wide transitional shots at low resolution and spend high settings only on close-ups and emotional beats — cuts render cost roughly 40%.

Step 6: Auto review & re-rolls

Raw acceptance rates hover around 30%. Volume production requires automated review (usability, character consistency, logic) — agent pipelines like Juirilu push acceptance above 90%. Solo creators can manually review only key shots.

Step 7: Dubbing & sound

ElevenLabs for English emotional range; Chinese productions favor Xunfei Zhizuo. Veo 3.1 and several newer models support native audio generation, including dialogue.

Step 8: Editing & international distribution

CapCut / Jianying for the final cut, captions and multi-platform export. Going overseas is the fastest-growing lane: the international AI-drama market is up ~550% YoY, and one-stop translation pipelines now handle bulk localization — humans focus on cultural taboos and local nuance.

One-line tool picks

  • Solo experiments: Xiaoyunque (~$6/mo) or free LLM + a structured workflow
  • Serialized teams: all-in-one platforms (Volcano Drama Engine, LibTV)
  • Technical teams: local ComfyUI + FLUX + Wan2.2, no watermarks

The real differentiator across this pipeline is not single-shot beauty — it's how much of Step 3 you engineer. Start by structuring your script.
